Void Siege — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 28 August 2026

Void Siege ("the game") is a single-player game published by Adam Cheesman ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

In short: the game has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not collect or transmit anything about you as you play. Information is only ever sent to us when you choose to fill in and submit the Report & Feedback form inside the game.

Information stored on your device
Your progress — runs played, best wave reached, cores, unlocked pilots and liveries, upgrades, achievements, season progress and your settings — is saved locally on your device. On mobile this uses the operating system's own app storage; in a web browser it uses local storage.

This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing site data in a browser, permanently deletes it.

Information sent when you submit feedback
The game includes a Report & Feedback form in its Settings menu. Nothing is sent unless you open that form and tap Submit. When you do, the following is transmitted to a private Google Sheet that we control:

What you type — your description of the bug or feedback, and the optional name field. This field is free text; anything you enter in it, including contact details you choose to add, is sent to us.
Whether the report is a bug or feedback, and the date and time of submission.
Technical details about your device — your browser or WebView user agent string (which typically identifies your device model and operating system version), screen and window size, display scaling, and whether you are using touch or a mouse.
The game's build version and which screen you were on.
Your game progress — total runs, best wave, cores, unlocked pilots and season tier.
Your current run, if one is in progress — wave, level, health, enemy count and selected pilot.
Whether the developer debug console was used in that session.
We use this solely to reproduce and fix problems and to understand feedback. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising or profiling.

What the game does not do
It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or postal address. (The feedback form has an optional free-text name field, which you may leave blank.)
It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
It contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
It does not track you across other apps or websites.
It does not require an internet connection to play, and works fully offline.
Service providers
Feedback submissions are delivered through Google Apps Script and stored in Google Sheets. As part of handling that request, Google may process technical information such as your IP address in line with its own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not record or retain your IP address ourselves.

If you install the game from Google Play or the Apple App Store, those stores handle the download and any purchases under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details.

How long we keep feedback
Feedback submissions are retained for as long as they remain useful for fixing and improving the game, and are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the feedback form, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights
Because the game has no accounts, we generally hold no information that identifies you. If you have submitted feedback and want the entry removed, or want to know what it contained, contact us with the approximate date and the name you used, and we will find and delete it.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or the CCPA to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact us to exercise them.

Changes to this policy
If we change what the game collects — for example by adding advertising or in-app purchases — we will update this policy and change the date at the top before those features go live.
